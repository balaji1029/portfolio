- NS-3 discrete-event simulations of enterprise Wi-Fi RRM for Arista's "RRM-Plus", which uses a dedicated extra radio for continuous spectrum sensing and non-Wi-Fi interferer classification.
- Client location triangulation from 802.11mc FTM round-trip times, for location-aware interference hot-spot detection.
- A channel-assignment protocol across 2.4/5/6 GHz under DFS/AFC and width constraints, maximising client throughput within change budgets and rollback guardrails.
