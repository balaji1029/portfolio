- A 3D modeller with custom GLSL shaders: spheres, cones, cylinders and boxes, hierarchical transforms, saved in a custom .mod format.
- Grew into a renderer with per-fragment Phong lighting from toggleable lights, an orbit camera, textures, and a fully articulated Minion in a textured room ([part 2](https://github.com/balaji1029/computer-graphics-assn2)).
- Then a keyframe animator: pose recording, smooth interpolation, Bézier camera paths and joint constraints — ending in a short animated film ([part 3](https://github.com/balaji1029/computer-graphics-assn3)).
